# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Argue about code, not people.

## Our pledge

We want participating in Riptide to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and take constructive feedback gracefully. A code review is about the patch.
- Accept responsibility, apologise to those affected by our mistakes, and learn from them.
- Remember that the person on the other end is volunteering their time.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as a physical or email address, without
  their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions and commit messages —
and whenever someone is officially representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers by opening an issue titled `conduct:` with no
details, and a private channel will be arranged. All reports will be reviewed promptly and
fairly, and the privacy and security of the reporter will be respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that do not align with this code of conduct, and will explain why when they do.
Repeated or severe violations may lead to a temporary or permanent ban from the project.
